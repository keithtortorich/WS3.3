Product

Tier Model and Agent Layer

Flat pricing, no ad markup, and the social media manager agent is bundled as the operating layer inside Business Manager.

TierPriceWhat it includesPositioning
Office Staff $497 / month Receptionist, service advisor, lead coordinator, reputation manager, website operations. Primary acquisition entry point. 30-day free trial.
Business Manager $2,497 / month Everything in Office Staff, plus sales consultant, marketing coordinator, operations manager, and the social media manager agent across Meta and additional platforms. Upgrade tier only. Flat platform fee.
Ad spend clarification: the customer pays all ad spend directly to the ad platforms. WebStaffr does not resell media. The agent optimizes toward booked jobs and ROI, not markups.
