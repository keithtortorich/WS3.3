# Pricing and Packaging — AI Department

## Packaging Strategy
Sell the AI department as a full team replacement, not as a feature list. Price by workers activated and usage volume. Clients should understand that they are buying coverage, response speed, and revenue recovery, not software seats.

## Tier Model

### Tier A: Front Desk Only
Included workers:
- Website Lead Follow-Up
- Missed-Call Text-Back

Best for:
- New clients who need immediate speed-to-lead fixes.
- Businesses with high website and call volume but no nurture or ads complexity.

Deliverables:
- Sub-5-minute website lead response
- Missed-call recovery within 60 seconds
- Basic GHL inbox cleanup
- Weekly reporting: first-response time, reply rate, recovery rate, appointments booked

Pricing:
- Monthly: $397–$497 per location
- Setup fee: $500 one-time
- Includes data import, GHL config, phone/webhook setup, 2-week tuning

Value framing:
- “We do not sell software. We answer your website leads and missed calls for you.”

### Tier B: Growth Department
Included workers:
- Front Desk Only
- Outreach Lead Nurturing

Best for:
- Businesses with cold lead lists or seasonal gaps.
- Clients needing more appointments than incoming lead volume can provide alone.

Deliverables:
- All Tier A features
- Batch outreach to cold, old, and unresponsive leads
- Reactivation campaigns
- Monthly reputation request campaign

Pricing:
- Monthly: $697–$897 per location
- Setup fee: $900 one-time

Value framing:
- “We book more appointments from the leads you already have.”

### Tier C: Full AI Department
Included workers:
- Growth Department
- Reputation Management
- Paid Ad Nurturing

Best for:
- Businesses running ads and needing ROI accountability.
- Practices or shops where reviews directly affect lead quality.

Deliverables:
- All Tier B features
- Review request and response system
- GBP maintenance
- Ad lead routing and nurture
- Weekly ad performance summary and recommendations

Pricing:
- Monthly: $997–$1,497 per location
- Setup fee: $1,500 one-time

Value framing:
- “Your full AI back office: staff your phone, leads, reviews, and ads with one subscription.”

## Usage Volume Pricing Add-Ons

Add overage tiers when clients exceed base volume:

| Metric | Included Base Volume | Overage Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Contacts | 2,000 | +$0.04 each |
| SMS | 5,000 | +$0.012 each |
| Outreach touches | 3,000 | +$0.025 each |
| Ad leads processed | 500 | +$0.50 each |
| Reviews managed | 200 | +$0.75 each |

Why this matters:
- Keeps entry price low.
- Prevents margin leaks on high-volume clients.
- Aligns pricing with actual resource use.

## Setup Fee Justification

Charge setup fees because the product value is in orchestration, not software. Setup blocks typically take:
- 40–90 minutes
- GHL config
- Phone/webhook integration
- Template/prompt customization
- Import and cleanup of lead database
- First-campaign planning and approval

Recommended setup fee anchors:
- Front Desk Only: $500
- Growth Department: $900
- Full AI Department: $1,500

Optional premium onboarding:
- Dedicated account manager, custom integrations, historical campaign analysis: +$1,000–$2,500

## Subscription Model Notes

### Billing
- Monthly recurring, auto-charged on G2C or charge flow.
- Annual pre-pay discount: 10–15% if client commits for 12 months.
- Minimum 3-month contract recommended to recover onboarding costs.

### Multi-Location
- Per-location pricing by default.
- Enterprise: $2,500–$5,000/month for 5+ locations with shared reporting.

### Add-On Services
- Ad spend management: 10–15% of ad spend or $300–$500/month minimum
- Custom AI worker or integration: quote by scope
- Monthly optimization/strategy call: $250/session or bundled

## Competitive Pricing Position

Competitor benchmarks:
- Reputation-only tools: $299–$450/location/month
- Outreach-only platforms: $300–$700/month
- Missed-call/text tools: standalone apps $50–$150/month
- Ad management agencies: month fee plus % of spend

Your advantage:
- Single vendor coordinates all five workers via shared GHL state.
- Lower total cost than buying 3–5 separate tools.
- Leads follow a unified journey instead of disjointed handoffs.

Price positioning:
- Front Desk: cheaper than Birdeye alone, with conversion-driving lead follow-up.
- Full AI Department: should land near or above the highest-tier competitor packages, because it replaces part-time employee behavior and drives direct revenue.

## Pricing Guardrails

- Never drop below setup fee recovery threshold unless the deal is clearly strategic or referral-based.
- Always retain exit rights to bill out-of-pocket API costs if a client cancels mid-cycle.
- Document what is and is not included:
  - included: normal operational volume, campaign management, support
  - not included: refunds, compensation, third-party ad spend, legal review of generated content, guarantees on appointments
- Keep contracts clear on AI-generated content liability.

## Sales Pitch Mapping to Tiers
Use this to sell faster:

- Pain: "We keep getting website leads but nobody follows up fast enough."
  Sell: Front Desk Only.

- Pain: "We have old leads in the spreadsheet but no time to call them."
  Sell: Growth Department.

- Pain: "Google reviews are killing us and we don't know if ads are worth the money."
  Sell: Full AI Department.

- Pain: "We need all of it but don't know where to start."
  Sell: Full AI Department with premium setup.
