## Notes on Methodology

All figures are drawn from publicly available 2025-2026 industry reports, platform benchmarks, and aggregated practitioner data. Where ranges are used (e.g., 25-40% recurring revenue), they reflect commonly cited top-quartile outcomes rather than universal averages. Primary sources (ServiceTitan, CallRail, Invoca, Jobber) are prioritized as they directly serve the HVAC/home services contractor market.
